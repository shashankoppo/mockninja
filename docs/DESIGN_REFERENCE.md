# MOCK NINJA: hero and theme reference

## Source of truth
Canvas: 1374 x 772 px. Base background near #0D0E15. The approved direction is a premium JEE preparation product with dark graphite surfaces, subtle violet and magenta gradients, restrained mint and amber accents, rounded outlined panels, and selective glassmorphism.

## Header
Floating rounded bar, 1px translucent border, dark graphite fill, graduation-cap logo, white “Mock” with violet-to-pink “Ninja”, compact navigation, pill search, pill language selector, rounded Login and Sign up buttons. Active navigation uses a violet-to-magenta underline.

## Hero
Left-aligned badge: “JEE Main Focused Platform”. Headline: “Focused Practice. Real Progress. Your JEE, Your Way.” Only “Real Progress.” uses the violet-to-pink-to-peach text gradient. Supporting copy: “High-quality mock tests, chapter-wise practice, PYQs, DPP and AI-powered doubt support — everything you need to improve and perform better in JEE Main.” Primary action: “Start Free Mock Test”. Secondary action: “Explore Test Series”.

## Feature stack
Four tilted glass cards: Mock Tests, Chapter Practice, PYQs & DPP, and AI Doubt Solver. Cards use violet, mint/teal, amber, and violet-pink accents with rounded-square icon tiles and circular arrow actions.

## Assurance row
NTA-style CBT interface, No ads, no distractions, Bilingual (EN / HI), and Safe & Secure. These are factual product descriptions only and must not imply government affiliation or unsupported security certification.

## Statistics bar
Four outlined columns: Registered Students, Tests Attempted, Practice Questions, and Test Series Available. Use an em dash until verified backend counts meet their configured thresholds. Never invent statistics.

## Responsive behavior
Desktop shows the floating header, hero, tilted feature stack, and statistics bar above the fold. At smaller widths the stack becomes a normal vertical list, buttons become full width, and statistics collapse to a grid without clipping.

## Theme tokens
--bg #101117; --header #111219; --surface #191923; --surface-elevated #22212D; --border #373443; --text #F5F3FA; --text-2 #B6B2C4; --violet #8B5CF6; --magenta #E879F9; --mint #55D6BE; --amber #F4B860; --danger #F0646E; --focus #C4B5FD.

## Guardrails
No NTA logo, national emblem, government wording, fake statistics, fake testimonials, fake security badges, camera claims, or unsupported official claims. Keep “Independent practice platform. Not affiliated with NTA.” in the public footer.
